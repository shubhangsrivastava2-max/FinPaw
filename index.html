Strategy 🧠

Compare mathematically defined investment scenarios.

Scenario inputs

This compares a monthly SIP with a lump sum of the same total contribution (monthly SIP × number of months). It is a simplified mathematical comparison, not a forecast of market performance.

Comparison results

Enter assumptions and compare to see calculated results.

How to interpret

Under a constant assumed rate, the two approaches can produce different ending values because money is invested for different lengths of time. Actual markets do not deliver a constant return, and neither method guarantees a profit.
